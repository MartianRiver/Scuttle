The <Term id="body">body</Term> can now mine stone (`get stone`) and then coal and iron, smelt the iron and make iron tools (`get iron`). It's the first thing it does that puts a hole in the world, so most of the work went into when it may not dig.

Each <Term id="block">block</Term> is dug only after a look-before-you-dig check: no lava or water against any face, nothing falling over it, nothing a player made within 3 blocks or up to 12 above it, and the body beside the block at its own level or below. For iron it only goes for <Term id="ore">ore</Term> with a face in the open, never ore buried in rock.

Three more rules came the same day: a wider list of blocks that count as made by a player, a map of places it won't dig for the rest of a run, and a route check before each dig, so a target it has no way to walk to is skipped.

All of this is checked in the <Term id="test-world">test world</Term> only.
