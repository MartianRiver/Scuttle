---
term: "Furnace"
short: "A block that cooks things. Put in raw iron and fuel such as coal and you get an iron ingot. Cooking like this is called smelting."
see: ["ore", "crafting-table"]
wiki: "https://minecraft.wiki/w/Furnace"
---

**Why it matters here.** The "get iron" job smelts at a furnace. It only uses one that has nothing but coal as fuel, so it never wastes someone else's stuff.
